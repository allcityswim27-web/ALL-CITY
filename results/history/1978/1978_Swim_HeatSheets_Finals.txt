WELCOME TO NAKOMA!

Nakoma Swim Club extends a warm welcome to the 1978
All City Pool Meet. We hope all swimmers and spectators
will enjoy our facilities and the meet. Please understand
the importance of observing the rules and confining all
activities to the designated areas, noting specifically
that the golf course is strictly off-limits. Also, in
traveling to and from the meet, please give consideration
to the neighborhood.

As host for this meet we are endeavoring to make it
the most successful ever. The cooperation of all swimmers
and spectators. will be expected and appreciated.

The Meet Director expresses his gratitude to the
excellent committee and staff whose efforts and cooperation
are essential to a successful meet. The understanding,
support and cooperation of the Nakoma Golf Club members
is acknowledged. A special thanks is given to ALL the
pool families.

THANK YOU!
